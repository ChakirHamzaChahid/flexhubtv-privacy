Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

FlexHubTV (technical name: PlexHubTV) is a media player client for Android TV and other Android devices. It connects to media servers and sources that you configure yourself (for example your own Plex Media Server or Jellyfin server, or a playlist you provide). It is developed and published by an independent developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Plex Inc., Jellyfin or any content provider. The app does not provide, host or distribute any media content.

Versions of the app

FlexHubTV is distributed as a Google Play version and as a direct-download version (sideload, GitHub). Both share the same code base; the differences that matter for your privacy are the default of the diagnostics switch (section 3) and a few extra features of the direct-download version (section 6).

Data We Collect

1. Connection data (stored on your device)

  • Plex authentication token, Plex account ID, Plex server identifiers (server ID, IP address or hostname)
  • Credentials or tokens for the other servers you add (for example Jellyfin), and the API keys you enter (metadata, subtitles, AI, Trakt)

Storage: tokens and API keys are stored on your device in Android encrypted storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences, AES-256-GCM). They are not sent to any server operated by the developer; they are only sent to the service they belong to, in order to authenticate you.

Purpose: to connect to your accounts and servers and access your media libraries.

2. Viewing data (stored on your device)

  • Media items you play (movie, show, episode) and playback progress (position, duration)
  • Favorites, watchlist, history, local profiles, downloads and app settings

Storage: this data is kept in the app's private storage on your device (local database, protected by the Android app sandbox). The app does not upload it to any server operated by the developer. Some optional features you enable send part of it to the third parties listed in section 5.

Purpose: to provide features such as "Continue Watching", history, recommendations and offline downloads.

3. Crash reports, diagnostics and performance

Providers: Sentry (crash and error reports, bug reports) and Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics, Performance Monitoring). One single switch, Settings → System & About → Crash reports, controls all of them.

Data sent when the switch is on:

  • Device model, Android version, app version, build type and install mode (Google Play or direct download)
  • Stack traces and error details (error messages are passed through a redaction filter before being sent to Sentry)
  • Screens visited and app events (for example navigation, playback started or failed, sign-in attempts), with technical identifiers truncated
  • Performance timings (start-up, screen rendering, network and playback timings)
  • A pseudonymous identifier: a truncated SHA-256 hash of your profile identifier (never the raw identifier), and a "kids profile" flag
  • Identifiers generated by Google's SDKs (installation / app-instance identifiers and, depending on your device settings, the Android advertising ID). We do not use them for advertising and we do not sell them.

Consent and default:

  • Google Play version: opt-in. Nothing is collected or sent to Sentry or Firebase until you turn the switch on.
  • Direct-download version: opt-out. The switch is on by default; you can turn it off at any time in the same place.
  • Turning the switch off stops the collection immediately, without restarting the app. A choice you have already made is kept when the app is updated.

Purpose: to detect crashes and improve the stability and performance of the app. These data are not used for advertising. Retention is determined by the providers' own settings and policies (see section 5).

4. Bug reports you send

If you use the "report a bug" feature, the report is sent to Sentry. It contains device information, the app version, the pseudonymous identifier above, your free-text description and the last 500 lines of the app log, in which passwords, tokens and keys are removed before sending. A report is only sent when you press the send button, and only while the switch of section 3 is on. Please do not put personal information in the description.

5. Network requests and optional third-party services

FlexHubTV communicates with:

  • Plex Inc. (plex.tv, and the Plex servers you configure) and the Jellyfin servers you configure
  • The Movie Database (TMDb) for metadata, images and ratings, and OMDb API for IMDb ratings: requests contain the title, year or external identifier of the item concerned, and an API key
  • Subtitle providers (OpenSubtitles, SubDL), if you use online subtitle search: requests contain the item's identifiers or title and an API key
  • Trakt (api.trakt.tv), only if you connect your Trakt account (device-code sign-in): the app then sends what you watch (history, watchlist, playback progress) to your Trakt account and reads it back
  • Moore Metrics (mooremetrics.com), only if you enter a Moore Metrics API key and use its recommendations: a short list (at most 10) of media titles is sent with your API key to obtain recommendations
  • Subtitle translation by AI, only if you choose it: the subtitle text to translate is sent to Groq or Google (Gemini) with the API key you entered, or to the self-hosted AI server whose address you provide
  • Recommendations by a self-hosted AI server, only if you configure one: media references (identifiers and titles) are sent to the server whose address you enter, which you operate
  • Google Play services: the app asks it to update its security provider (no data from the app)

These services have their own privacy policies, for example:

  • Plex: https://www.plex.tv/about/privacy-legal/
  • TMDb: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy
  • OMDb: http://www.omdbapi.com/legal.htm
  • Trakt: https://trakt.tv/privacy
  • Google (Firebase, Gemini): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Sentry: https://sentry.io/privacy/
  • Groq: https://groq.com/privacy-policy/

6. Extra features of the direct-download version only

The direct-download version (not the Google Play version) additionally contains: an update check against the GitHub releases API (api.github.com), which, like any web request, exposes your IP address and user agent to GitHub; and extra media sources that you configure yourself (for example IPTV playlists or accounts, a self-hosted server, or third-party websites). When you use them, the app communicates directly with the provider you chose, and that provider's own privacy policy applies. The developer does not receive these requests.

Data Storage and Retention

  • Sensitive data (tokens, API keys) is stored locally using Android's encrypted storage; other app data stays in the app's private storage on your device.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can delete all local data by uninstalling the app or by using your device's "Clear data" option.
  • Diagnostic data held by Sentry and Firebase is kept according to those providers' settings and policies.

Children's Privacy

FlexHubTV is not directed at children under 13. The app has local profiles with a parental-control option; any content restrictions otherwise depend on your own servers and profiles.

Your Rights

  • On-device data: you can delete all app data from your device at any time.
  • Diagnostics: you can turn the switch of section 3 off at any time. You may also contact the developer to ask about, or ask for the deletion of, diagnostic data relating to you; because these data are pseudonymous, we may ask you for information that helps locate them (for example the identifier included in a bug report you sent).
  • Accounts of third parties (Plex, Jellyfin servers, Trakt, TMDb and others): managed by those services; you can revoke FlexHubTV's access from your account on each of them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.
